## Overview

Duluin Gajian lets employees withdraw part of their earned wages early and pay bills in the app. The campaign engine lets marketing and operations run promotions — vouchers, cashback, referral programmes and bulk point grants — for specific client companies, without help from engineering.

## My contribution

- Designed and built the campaign engine as part of the team, starting during my internship.
- Implemented the campaign types, their targeting, and the points ledger behind them.

## Skills applied

- **Rule engine design.** Every campaign type goes through the same checks: active period, total quota, per-user limit, minimum amount and product type.
- **Two-phase rewards.** Usage is recorded when a transaction is requested, and rewards are credited only after it succeeds, so failed transactions earn nothing.
- **Abuse prevention.** Referral rules block self-referral and repeat redemption.
- **Resumable bulk jobs.** Bulk point grants run as queued jobs that continue where they stopped if interrupted.
- **Auditability.** A points ledger records every credit and debit with its status and expiry.
- **Access control.** Campaign management actions are protected by role-based permissions.

## Outcome

- Marketing and operations can run targeted promotions without engineering help.
- Every usage, referral and point movement is recorded, so rewards can be audited.

## Tech stack

Laravel, Sanctum, Spatie Permission · MySQL · Redis queues (Horizon)
